Throw Restock

Throw-only restocking mod for Supermarket Simulator. Throw an open box at a shelf to stock it, and throw empty boxes at the trash bin to dispose of them.

Version: 1.3.6

File: ThrowRestock-v1.3.6.zip

SHA256: 670DEC72FA0EC7C918CDDBEA4FECC93B8D6D659F7E3047768BFC9710BC0898ED

Install by extracting the zip and placing ThrowRestock.dll into:

Supermarket Simulator\BepInEx\plugins\ThrowRestock\

Each player who wants the throw behavior should install the mod.
